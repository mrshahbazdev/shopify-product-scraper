Privacy Policy — Shopify Product Scraper

Shopify Product Scraper — Free & Unlimited, version 1.2.0

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Shopify Product Scraper is a browser extension that downloads the public product catalog of a Shopify store chosen by the user and exports it as CSV, Excel (.xlsx) or JSON. This policy explains what the extension does with data — in short: it collects nothing about you.

What the extension accesses

When you ask it to scrape a store, the extension requests that store's public product feed (products.json) — the same product data any visitor's browser can already see on the store (titles, prices, variants, options, SKUs, stock quantities, images and product URLs). It never accesses a store's private or admin data, and it never reads your browsing history or data from other websites.

What the extension collects — nothing

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No personal information (no name, email, address or payment details).
  • No browsing history and no data from websites other than the store you choose to scrape.
  • No account, no sign-up and no login of any kind.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifiers.
  • No data is sent to any server — third-party or our own. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Permissions used — and why

  • activeTab — to detect whether the tab you are on is a Shopify store and read its address, so the "Use this tab's store" button works.
  • storage — to remember two local settings only, on your own device: the last store address you entered and the last product count. Nothing else is stored.
  • Host access (https://*/*) — so the extension can request the public products.json feed of any Shopify store you choose to scrape. It is used only for that request.

Where your data is processed

Locally, in your browser. The scraped product data is held in memory only while the extension popup is open and is downloaded to a file on your device only when you click Download. The extension has no server, no database and no cloud component.

Children's privacy

The extension collects no data from anyone, including children.
