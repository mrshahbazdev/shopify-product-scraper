Shopify Product Scraper

Free & Unlimited — scrape every product from any Shopify store.

Shopify Product Scraper pulls the full product catalog from any Shopify store and lets you download it as CSV (Shopify import format), Excel (.xlsx) or JSON. No product limit, no account, no license. Free forever.

How it works

  • Open any Shopify store and click the extension icon — it detects the store automatically.
  • Or paste any store URL and click Scrape.
  • Every product is pulled — 100 or 5,000, no limit.
  • Download CSV, Excel or JSON, or copy all product URLs at once.

Get it

Available on the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons (links added once the listings are live).

Privacy

The extension only reads Shopify's public product data and collects nothing about you. Read the full Privacy Policy.
